Lists & Indexing

How Python finds an item by its position — and why slices stop one step early.

1A list is a row of numbered boxes

Every item in a list sits at a position called its index. Counting starts at 0 from the left, or at -1 from the right.

Key idea

A slice start:stop includes start but excludes stop. So its length is always stop − start.
